# Mechanical Design: Queen Seraphine Valehart in Stronghold Crusader

## High concept
Seraphine should feel like a ruler whose psychology becomes gameplay:
- she avoids straightforward brute-force trades early
- she pressures, circles, and probes
- she punishes openings rather than forcing them immediately
- she uses mobility and precision kills
- she creates a sense of harassment before annihilation

Her army identity:
- primary field identity: horse archers (attack unit patrol scaling up to 240)
- signature execution identity: assassins (20 base assassins + infnitely scalign assassins in main with attackmax default 0 so scalign after horseearchers)
- supporting just a few slingers for defense, and mainly assassins for raids
- triggered by hunters

She should feel very aggressive right from the start getting cotnrol of the battlefield, domianting it with her horsearchers
1. irritates you,
2. destabilizes you,
3. finds your breach,
4. ends you personally.

---

## Economic profile
### Preferred economy
- strong wood focus early and spamming gold makign economy + some stone for gardens
- moderate food stability, often spams hunters and double rations, later survives jsut on positive fearfactor and religion alone
- getting iron very late and sellign almost all of it (keeps just 3 for eweapon production, to sell iron armors)
- sells also pitch aggressively
- very very lategame might get stables, based on the castle, then entirely switches to knight raid superiority

### Why this makes sense
Horse archers need mobility and recurring production, so her economy should not hinge on a slow heavy-armor ramp from the start. She should feel agile and opportunistic rather than industrial.

Economically, she should resemble a lord who wants to get "online" faster than a heavy knight lord, but who remains more dangerous over time because of pressure and sneaky precision units.

---

## AI personality translated into mechanics
### Key mechanical themes
- harassment over frontal slugging
- mobility over armor
- attrition over clean battle lines
- vulnerability testing
- opportunistic assassination spikes
- emotionally "spiky" behavior: sometimes measured, sometimes abrupt

### Battlefield personality
Her forces should:
- circle and pester
- bait chasing
- shred exposed workers and weak skirmishers
- exploit wall gaps and side paths
- create pressure while keeping distance
- punish static defenses with maneuver rather than heavy tanking

---

## Early game design
## Strategic identity
In the early game she should be annoying, mobile, and slightly under-armored, but not weak. She should not all-in too early. Instead, she should build an economy that transitions quickly into harassment.

### Early priorities
- wood production
- food stabilization
- horsearchers patrolling aroudn farms
- money making machine
- shut down enemy light raiders with assassins and slingers

### Early troop behavior
- small archer groups or skirmishing forces
- early horse archer trickle if available
- workers and weakly defended outer eco become preferred targets
- little appetite for direct fortress assault
- may test undefended flanks rather than commit centrally

### Mechanical purpose
This makes her distinct from blunt rush lords. She behaves like a predator learning the map:
- Where are your weak resources?
- Are your workers exposed?
- Can you be baited into chasing?
- Are your towers undermanned?

### Stronghold Crusader fit
This works well because early eco harassment is highly valuable. A player who over-expands outer woodcutters, quarries, or farms becomes the perfect target for her mounted harassment. She should teach the player that greed invites pain.

---

## Mid game design
## Strategic identity
This is Seraphine's strongest and most iconic phase.

By mid game, she should become a true map-control menace:
- horse archer groups increase
- attack waves become more coordinated
- assassins begin appearing in meaningful numbers
- she probes walls, edges, ladders, and side routes
- she becomes much more confident once she senses weakness

### Mid game troop composition
Primary:
- horse archers
- archers or cross support depending on balance choice
Secondary:
- assassins
- spearmen/pikemen for support or screening
Optional:
- small macemen or swordsmen group only if needed for breach follow-up
Minimal:
- heavy knights unless mod balance specifically wants them

### Mid game AI behavior
- circles around enemy outer economy
- focuses isolated units
- punishes poorly defended towers
- softens defenders with ranged mobility
- sends assassins when:
  - wall sections are lightly guarded
  - ladders or approach routes exist
  - gatehouse area is vulnerable
  - chaos already exists from ranged pressure

### Why assassins fit mechanically
Assassins in Stronghold Crusader are strongest when the enemy is distracted, stretched thin, or poorly layered. Horse archers create exactly that environment:
- defenders reposition constantly
- outer towers get thinned
- player attention gets dragged outward
- panic makes stealth pushes more likely to succeed

Her gameplay loop becomes:
1. harass
2. distract
3. thin
4. infiltrate
5. decapitate

That is extremely on-theme.

## Late game design
## Strategic identity
Late game Seraphine should not simply become "another heavy lord." She should remain herself.

That means her late game should scale in pressure and cruelty, not in generic armor mass alone.

### Late game strengths
- relentless horse archer presence
- strong control of open terrain
- repeated infiltration attempts
- good ability to punish outer economy even late
- dangerous to players who rely too much on static defenses
- capable of sudden lord-kill attempts if one path opens

### Late game weaknesses
- less efficient in an honest, prolonged heavy infantry slugfest
- can struggle against extremely layered anti-ranged turtle castles if she lacks siege adaptation
- relies on map space for full cavalry value
- vulnerable if forced into narrow, attritional choke fights without breach opportunities

### Late game behavior recommendations
- add more coordinated split attacks
- continue ranged pressure on exposed resource lines
- send assassins after each distraction spike, not randomly
- occasionally use small siege support to force defensive movement
- target wells of stability: engineers, oil setups, exposed tower archers, isolated economic nodes

### What makes this fun
Her late game should feel psychologically exhausting:
you are never safe enough to ignore your eco,
never calm enough to ignore a side path,
never certain that the next pressure wave is "just harassment."

## Difficulty tuning knobs
To balance her for different AI levels or mods, tune these variables:

### Easier version
- slower horse archer mass
- fewer assassins
- less synchronized attack waves
- more predictable targeting
- lower eco aggression

### Standard version
- consistent mobile harassment
- assassins as real mid-game threat
- intelligent outer-eco targeting
- respectable but not overwhelming castle defense

### Brutal version
- very active harassment cycles
- repeated multi-angle horse archer pressure
- assassins used whenever visual weakness appears
- strong timing overlap between harassment and infiltration
- punishes greedy expansion immediately

## Unit preference summary
### High preference
- horse archers
- assassins
- archers

### Medium preference
- spearmen / pikemen
- occasional macemen or swordsmen for structure collapse support

### Low preference
- knights
- heavy static melee blobs
- slow, prestige-heavy armies that do not match her identity

---

## Targeting logic
Seraphine should prefer:
1. exposed workers
2. outer economy
3. isolated ranged defenders
4. weak towers
5. thin wall segments
6. breachable paths
7. the enemy lord if chaos is already present

She should not prefer:
- frontal assault into concentrated kill-zones unless she has already destabilized them
- pointless attrition against well-prepared heavy defenses
- wasting assassins on impossible routes

---

## Defensive behavior
Her castle defense should not be purely passive.

She should:
- use towers with ranged emphasis
- keep mobile reserve units
- launch counter-harassment when pressured
- use assassins defensively in surprise counter-infiltration if the mod allows flavorful scripting
- defend with layered inconvenience rather than just brute wall thickness

This is important: even on defense, she should feel slippery and dangerous.

---

## Why she fits Stronghold Crusader
Stronghold Crusader rewards strong identity in AI lords. Seraphine fits because she offers:
- memorable battlefield personality
- clear tactical signature
- visually coherent army style
- strong synergy between lore and mechanics
- high frustration factor in a good way
- a fresh experience compared to pure turtlers or pure rush lords

She is not "the strongest at everything."
She is "the most emotionally invasive opponent."

That is much more interesting.

---

## One-line mechanical summary
Seraphine is a mobility-pressure assassin lord whose horse archers create the chaos that her infiltrators finish.
